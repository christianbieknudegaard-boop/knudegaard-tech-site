How to play

One finger. Easy to learn in seconds, hard to put down.

Swipe to steerSlide your finger left and right. Your fish always swims up.
Dodge everythingHooks, nets, lobster traps, jellyfish and the dreaded trawler. Brush past a hook for a close-call bonus.
Grow biggerEat pearls and snatch the bait to grow. A bigger fish earns a bigger pearl multiplier.
Spell FRENZYCollect all six letters and go wild: faster, stronger and worth more.
