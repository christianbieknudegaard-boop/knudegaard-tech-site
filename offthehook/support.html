Support

We read every message and usually reply within a day or two.

Write to us through the contact form. If something went wrong, tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Playing

How do I steer?

Put a finger anywhere on the screen and slide it left or right. The fish follows your finger and always swims upward on its own.

What ends a run?

Hooks, nets, lobster traps, jellyfish and trawlers all cost a heart. When you run out of hearts, you're caught. Extra hearts come from the Extra Fin upgrade and from fish like the Sea Turtle.

How do I get more pearls?

Collect pearls on the way up, snatch the bait off hooks, brush past hooks for close-call bonuses and grow bigger for a higher multiplier. Daily missions and the daily gift give pearls too.

What is FRENZY?

Collect the letters F-R-E-N-Z-Y during a run. When you have all six, your fish goes into a frenzy for a short time: faster, stronger and worth more.

How do fish powers work?

22 of the 24 fish have their own power, shown on each fish in the shop. Some work all the time (like a smaller hitbox or double pearls), some protect you once per run (like the Puffer), and some recharge (like the Shark's bite). Powers that recharge or can be used up show a small badge at the top of the screen during a run.

How do I unlock a fish?

Most fish are bought with pearls in the shop. Some are earned by playing, for example by swimming far or making fish friends. The shop shows how far along you are.

Full version

What does the full version include?

All 12 epic and legendary fish, double pearls from every run and 1000 bonus pearls right away. It is a single payment, not a subscription, and the game never shows ads either way.

I bought the full version but it's not active

Open the Shop and tap “Restore purchase”. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If the purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), it turns on as soon as it's approved.

Can my family use it?

If Family Sharing is turned on, family members can restore the full version on their own devices.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Game Center

The leaderboard doesn't open

Open the Settings app → Game Center and make sure you're signed in, then open the game again and tap “Ranks”.

Data

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress is stored on your iPhone and moves with it when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup. If you delete the game, the progress is deleted too. The full version can always be restored.

What do you know about me?

Nothing. Off the Hook has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy for details.
