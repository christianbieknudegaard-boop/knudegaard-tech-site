Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Knudegaard Tech does not collect personal data through this website, apart from what you write in the contact form. The site uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics.

Each app has its own privacy policy, which you can find on the app’s website. Our apps share a few principles: no ads, and we never sell or share your data with others.

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to us by email through the Web3Forms service. We only use this information to reply to you and delete the enquiry once it is resolved. Privacy questions can be sent through the contact form.

Data controller: Knudegaard Tech, Eineråsen 21D, 4790 Lillesand, Norway.
